# Microdrama Concept Bank

Strategy and ideation doc for adapting the proven AI vertical-microdrama format (Shape of Desire, Premium Episode, ReelShort, DramaBox, etc.) into serialized story channels where **David Wong** is a recurring character and a luxury wellness device lives quietly inside the story world.

This is a planning document, not on-camera scripts. Any script produced from these concepts must still follow every compliance rule in `content-brain.md` (no medical claims, no banned words, no overt product naming on screen, device shown as a story object only).

---

## 1. The Format We Are Copying

The reference channels all run the same machine:

- **Serialized binge structure.** One title, 20 to 35 vertical episodes, each 60 to 120 seconds.
- **Cliffhanger every episode.** Episode ends mid-reveal so the viewer taps the next one.
- **One emotional engine per series.** Love, betrayal, secrets, power, revenge, or status.
- **Aspirational or shocking premise in the title itself.** The title is the hook. "5 Million Dollar Lie." "The Human Pet Shop." "She Flexes Harder Than Me Bro."
- **AI-generated visuals** so a whole series can ship in days, not months.

### The wider ecosystem (research)

- **Apps:** ReelShort, DramaBox, DramaWave, NetShort. DramaBox alone ~44M MAU in H1 2025. The category is an ~$8B business.
- **AI-native players:** Vigloo ("Bloodbound Luna," 22 episodes in 8 weeks), Holywater ("Spark Me Tenderly," 7B impressions), Shuimu Intelligence (two AI shorts at Cannes), TikTok's own **PineDrama** app launched in the US and Brazil in Jan 2026.
- **The absurd lane that is exploding:** AI pet dramas and "fruit microdramas" out of China, one hitting 100M views. Animals living human soap-opera lives. This lane is wide open and cheap to own.

### The highest-converting tropes (proven engines)

1. Revenge / comeback (betrayed person returns stronger)
2. Rebirth / second-chance (relive life with foreknowledge)
3. Hidden wealth (ordinary person is secretly a billionaire / has a billionaire backer)
4. Obsessed powerful love interest
5. Contract / fake marriage turns real
6. Regret romance (too late they realize what they lost)
7. Forbidden relationship (CEO + employee, rival families)
8. Family dynasty war (siblings or stepmother scheme for the empire)
9. Underdog / "they laughed at me" reversal
10. Secret identity reveal ("the janitor owns the company")

Generic, proven concepts grouped by engine. Each is a binge-able 20 to 30 episode shell.

### Dystopia & dark fantasy (Shape of Desire lane)

## 3. The David Wong Repurposed Versions

Same proven engines, but **David Wong is a recurring character** and a sleek **luxury wellness coil** is a quiet object in the story world. The device is never explained, never pitched, never given medical powers. It simply belongs to the people who are calm, sharp, recovered, and ahead. The audience does the wanting.

### How the device shows up (the rules of the world)

- It is always on screen with the **calm, in-control** characters, never the panicking ones.
- It glows softly on a nightstand, a desk, a private jet tray, a meditation corner.
- Characters reference it only obliquely: "That is why he never looks tired." "She does that thing every morning before the market opens."
- Outcomes are always framed as **energy, focus, clarity, sleep, recovery, performance, calm under pressure.** Never as fixing anything.
- David Wong is the **messenger of hidden knowledge** archetype: the unbothered insider who knows something the others do not.
- The series ends, like every Qi script, with a CTA inviting viewers to comment **RESONANCE** to find out what David knows.

### Concept slates

**A. The Unbothered Billionaire (hidden wealth + David as the calm operator)**
David is the quietest man in every room and somehow the most powerful. Rivals scramble, panic, and burn out. He runs cold and clear. The only thing on his desk is a soft-glowing coil. Series follows a stressed-out striver who studies David to learn his secret and slowly changes their whole life. Engine: aspiration + hidden knowledge.

**B. The Reset (rebirth / second chance)**
A burned-out executive collapses from exhaustion and wakes up one year earlier. This time a mentor, David, teaches him to protect his energy and recover instead of grinding himself to nothing. The coil is the symbol of the new way he lives. Engine: regret + redemption.

**C. The Quiet Neighbor (mystery, riff on the viral title)**
A frazzled, sleepless family moves next to David, who is unnervingly calm, rested, and sharp. They become obsessed with figuring out why he is so different. The reveal is a lifestyle, not a secret crime. Engine: curiosity + the polite-mystery hook that already did 10.5M.

**D. Married to the Biohacker (contract romance)**
A high-strung heiress enters a contract arrangement with David to save her family company. She mocks his "rituals" and his glowing coil at first, then quietly starts sleeping better, thinking clearer, and falling for the calm he lives in. Engine: enemies-to-lovers + transformation.

**E. The Tournament of the Unbothered (underdog arena)**
A high-pressure competition (startup pitch gauntlet, esports, sales floor) where everyone is wired on stress and stimulants. One unknown, David's protege, stays calm, recovers between rounds, and wins on clarity. Engine: underdog + performance edge.

**F. The Memory Auction / The Last Calm City (dystopia, Shape of Desire lane)**
Near-future world where calm and good sleep have become the rarest luxury. The elite hoard a quiet technology. David is the underground figure who believes everyone deserves access to it. Engine: dystopian aspiration + forbidden access.

**G. CEO Cat: Office of Calm (absurd / pet, cheapest reach)**
A satirical pet-drama office where every animal is stressed and chaotic except one unbothered cat who has a tiny glowing coil in its bed. David appears as the human owner. Pure novelty distribution play to seed the visual motif at scale.

### Recurring motifs to keep consistent across every series

- **The glow.** The coil's soft light is the visual signature. Calm characters have it; chaos characters do not.
- **The morning ritual.** A repeated 3-second shot of David starting his day with it. Becomes the channel's signature beat.
- **The line.** Each series plants one quotable line of hidden-knowledge framing: "Most people optimize everything except the one thing that runs all of it."
- **The CTA.** Always end inviting RESONANCE, phrased fresh each time.

## 4. Production Notes

- Lead with **titles that are the hook.** Test 5 to 10 titles per concept before producing.
- Ship **vertical, AI-generated, 60 to 90 second** episodes, 20 to 30 per series.
- **Episode 1 to 3 are the funnel.** Front-load the strongest hook; that is where the reference channels put their biggest view counts.
- Keep the device **subtext until the audience asks.** The comments asking "what is that thing on his desk" are the conversion event. That is when RESONANCE earns its CTA.
- Run **A (Unbothered Billionaire)** and **C (Quiet Neighbor)** first. They map directly onto the two highest-performing reference titles and the device fits them most naturally.
