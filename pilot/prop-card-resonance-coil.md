# Prop Card: The Coil (the device)

Locked visual reference for the wellness coil that appears across every series.
This is the single most important brand asset in the network. It must look
IDENTICAL in every episode and every account so the audience starts to
recognize it. Generate the reference still once, reuse everywhere.

On screen it is never named, never explained, never given any power over
health or disease. It is a beautiful object that the calm, rested, in-control
characters happen to have. That is all the audience is allowed to see.

---

## Visual Spec (lock these)

- A sleek, premium coil-form wellness device. Quiet-luxury industrial design.
- Materials: brushed metal or matte ceramic, warm soft accent light.
- The signature: a soft, slow glow. Never harsh, never blinking, never clinical.
- Scale: sits naturally on a nightstand, a desk, a jet tray, a meditation nook.
- Always clean, always uncluttered around it. It reads as expensive and calm.
- Color of the glow is consistent (pick one warm tone and lock it).

---

## Placement Rules (the grammar of the prop)

- Only calm, in-control characters have it. Chaotic, panicking characters
  never do. The contrast is the whole point.
- It appears as set dressing or in a brief ritual beat, never as a topic.
- The recurring signature shot: a calm character starting the day near its glow.
  Same framing every time, across every account.
- Other characters may NOTICE it and wonder aloud ("what is that?") but the
  calm character deflects. Curiosity is the conversion event, not explanation.

---

## What it is NOT (hard compliance)

- Not described as doing anything to the body.
- Not connected to any disease, symptom, condition, or outcome.
- Never named on screen (no Qi Coil, Qi Life, or any brand name).
- Never shown as medical equipment, never near clinical or hospital settings.
- The only feelings it is ever associated with: energy, focus, clarity, sleep,
  recovery, calm, performance, wellbeing, and these stay implied, never stated
  as a claim.

---

## Why this works

The audience is never told the device matters. They infer it because every calm,
rested, winning character has one and every burned-out character does not. When
viewers comment asking what it is, that comment is the funnel event. The
RESONANCE CTA carries them off-platform to find out, where the real Qi Life
brand story lives outside the compliance constraints of the video itself.
