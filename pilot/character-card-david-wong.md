# Character Card: David Wong

Locked reference for visual and voice consistency across every account and
series in the network. Generate the reference stills ONCE per season from this
card, then reference those images in every shot prompt. Do not improvise the
look episode to episode.

This is the in-story character. He is calm, unbothered, and quietly powerful:
the insider who knows something the others do not.

---

## Identity

- Name in-story: David Wong
- Archetype: the unbothered insider / messenger of hidden knowledge
- Energy: confident, casual, curious, never rushed, never loud
- He never lectures, never sells, never panics. Stillness is his power.

---

## Visual Spec (lock these for image/video gen)

- Asian man, late 30s to early 40s, sharp and healthy, well rested.
- Lean athletic build. Relaxed posture. Unhurried movements.
- Short, neat dark hair. Clean or lightly stubbled. Calm, knowing eyes.
- Wardrobe: quiet luxury. Matte black or charcoal. Fine knitwear, tailored
  minimalist pieces, no logos, no loud color. One subtle premium watch.
- Settings he belongs in: glass penthouse, private study, dawn-lit balcony,
  quiet corner office, minimalist meditation nook. Always calm, never cluttered.
- Lighting: soft, warm, cinematic. He is usually the calmest lit thing in frame.

## Voice Spec (lock one voice ID)

- One ElevenLabs (or equivalent) voice ID, used on every account.
- Tone: low, calm, measured, warm, lightly amused. Never hyped.
- Emotional mapping: mostly composed; rare, controlled intensity for stakes.
- This voice IS the brand sound. Never swap it between series.

---

## Behavior Rules (how he reads on screen)

- He is always the calm pole. Other characters scramble; he does not.
- He has a quiet morning beat with the coil prop. Same 2 to 3 second shot,
  reused as a signature across episodes and accounts.
- He drops one quotable hidden-knowledge line per arc, e.g.
  "Most people optimize everything except the one thing that runs all of it."
- He never explains the device. If asked, he deflects with calm and curiosity.

---

## Hard Compliance (applies to him too)

- He never makes a medical claim, never names a disease, never promises an
  outcome, never names a real product.
- Any benefit implied around him is framed only as energy, focus, clarity,
  sleep, recovery, calm, performance, wellbeing.
